Why the solutions are the thing you're actually buying

Four of the thirteen exercises don't have the answer the lab text implies, and those four are the most useful pages in the bundle. The sun-facing radiator penalty is 3.06×, not the 2× stated. One exercise has no answer at all — the bottleneck you're told to chase never becomes binding, because a third constraint overtakes it first. Weight repair never fails, because it's a full restore rather than error correction, which means the real limits are somewhere else entirely. And the package's own crossover figure is operating-point dependent and disagrees with itself; the workbook shows you how to get the right number.

Every figure in the material is computed by the packages and re-checked against a fresh install. Every code block runs in continuous integration weekly, so nothing silently rots when a package updates.

Honest scope

These are first-order models with public default parameters. They'll kill a bad architecture cheaply and make sure the expensive analysis starts from the right question. They are not a substitute for detailed thermal analysis with real geometry, full orbital mechanics including beta-angle variation, link budgets with real antenna patterns and margin, or radiation qualification against your actual parts.

Default cost figures are public list rates and they date within months. Override every one of them before quoting a result to anyone.
